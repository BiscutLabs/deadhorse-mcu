## Numbers

All reported simulation/proof/check outcomes are **measured** at baseline commit `a280df4fa17516a2ac8507628a6f12e3c4128895` plus the emitted source hashes in their receipts, using the pinned environment. GF180 results use Yosys 0.71+34/OpenSTA 3.2.0, the pinned 7-track SS/125 C/3.0 V library, ideal clocks and mapped cell area; they are not routed or board signoff.

## Deviations from the brief

The user-approved selective conversion composes Chisel endpoints with the retained SIE/PHY rather than retaining the original hardwired top. An explicit reset-and-flush handoff replaces an unobservable quiescence requirement for ownership changes. Timing-driven pipelines preserve the public protocol cadence and are checked by wire tests. The PHY exception does not waive clock-domain crossing, timing, electrical or physical qualification.

T19 is not complete: packet proofs, full integrated wrapper clock-domain timing and a frozen passing aggregate/CI remain open. There is no approved proof deferral, relaxed delay limit, packet-size change or shipping VID/PID assignment.
