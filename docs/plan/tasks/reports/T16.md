The independent pin peer measures **771 HF edges** of CS selection for command,
dummy and 14 payload bytes at half-period H3: `(16 * 16 + 1) * 3`.
Estimated durations from that measured count and assumed HF frequencies are
**85.666667 us at 9 MHz**, **64.25 us at 12 MHz**, and **32.125 us at 24 MHz**,
each below the 150 us requirement. Estimated SCLK at assumed 24 MHz is
`24 / (2 * 3) = 4 MHz`. These are digital model results, not board measurements.

## Open issues

- Frame formal results cover 128 edges, FIFO 2, H3, one arbitrary TX byte and
  scripted setup in each mode; they do not prove RX, timestamps or arbitrary
  firmware. The denied-access proof covers owner zero/all non-owner tags and
  observable disabled state, not active ownership transfer.
- Shared MISO synchronization assumes external data settles before the first
  HF edge after launch and remains stable until sampling. Pads, board delays,
  metastability/MTBF, clock and reset distribution need physical qualification.
- Firmware must honor sensor-specific idle gaps and sensor initialization.
  Timestamp comes from the integration's coherent same-HF time input; no timer,
  DRDY policy, DMA engine, pin mux, final address or SoC wiring is implemented.
- T13/S1 and inherited T14 formal deferrals remain open. No gate is checked.
