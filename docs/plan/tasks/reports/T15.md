not qualify the physical reset distribution tree. Independent review checked
the recurrence against closed-form bit boundaries for every legal increment
and both TX/RX seeds. That arithmetic review is not a temporal RTL proof.

## Open issues

- TX formal is bounded to 160 edges, FIFO depth two, increment 8192, normal
  polarity and one arbitrary byte under scripted configuration. It does not
  prove arbitrary firmware, RX, all baud rates or every FIFO size. Simulation
  supplies broader protocol evidence. The addition lemma does not prove the
  temporal byte-pipeline transformation.
- Denied-access proof uses owner zero and all non-owner tags, FIFO depth two,
  and observable disabled-state preservation. It does not prove ownership
  transfer while work is active; integration must drain/block that transfer.
- AON wake proof is digital and assumes initial reset plus settled armed/active
  samples. Metastability, clock startup and physical reset recovery/removal are
  not proved. Wake-only requires the documented long preamble and retry;
  receive mode keeps HF running for unsolicited lossless reception.
- DMA engine, gateway, power-controller handoff, pin mux, final addresses and
  SoC wiring remain later briefs. T13/S1 and inherited T14 deferrals remain open.
- Loaded skills contain historical unbuilt-tool and fixed-domain caveats;
  current repository contracts and pinned working flows take precedence.

Historical failed timing receipts remain under `build/t15/timing-initial`,
`timing-03` and `timing-bank-04` through `timing-bank-06`; final measurements
do not erase them. `formal-initial` failed its wake fault-model assertion-count
check before a solver result; it is not accepted proof evidence.
